## Recommended countermeasures

### CM015: Provide left-turn lane on one major-road approach

Providing a left-turn lane on one major-road approach addresses the high frequency of left-turn opposing crashes. The cited CMF 0.72 (CMF Clearinghouse 267) is a direct crash-type match for KABCO injury crashes, but requires engineering review for prerequisites and does not directly apply to fatal or serious injury crashes.

- Evidence: CMF 0.72, crash types All, severity K;A;B;C; engineering review only.
- Source: CMF Clearinghouse record 267, https://cmfclearinghouse.fhwa.dot.gov/detail.php?facid=267
- Limitation: Adjusted SE is 0.06. This record supports one added left-turn lane, not right-turn lanes or a protected left-turn signal. KABC is not KA only.

### CM019: Modify change plus clearance intervals to studied ITE 1985 practice

Modifying change plus clearance intervals to the studied ITE 1985 practice may address rear-end and left-turn related crashes. The cited CMF 0.92 (CMF Clearinghouse 380) covers all crash types and severities, but needs engineering review for area setting and does not directly apply to fatal or serious injury crashes.

- Evidence: CMF 0.92, crash types All, severity All; engineering review only.
- Source: CMF Clearinghouse record 380, https://cmfclearinghouse.fhwa.dot.gov/detail.php?facid=380
- Limitation: Adjusted SE is 0.10. HSM flags use with caution; interval includes 1.0. Historic treatment definition is not a current signal-timing design prescription or isolated yellow-only effect.
