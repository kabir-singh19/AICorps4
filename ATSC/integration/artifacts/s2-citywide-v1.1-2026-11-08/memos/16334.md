## Recommended countermeasures

### CM015: Provide left-turn lane on one major-road approach

Providing a left-turn lane on one major-road approach is supported by broader evidence for urban signalized intersections with similar traffic volumes. The cited CMF 0.72 (CMF Clearinghouse 267) is an aggregate for all crash types and severities, not a direct match for left-turn opposing or fatal/serious injury crashes, so engineering review is needed.

- Evidence: CMF 0.72, crash types All, severity K;A;B;C; engineering review only.
