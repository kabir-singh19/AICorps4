## Recommended countermeasures

### CM015: Provide left-turn lane on one major-road approach

Providing a left-turn lane on one major-road approach is supported by broader evidence for four-leg urban signalized intersections with similar traffic volumes. The cited CMF 0.72 (CMF Clearinghouse 267) is an aggregate for all crash types and KABCO severities, not a direct match for left-turn-opposing or fatal/serious crashes; engineering review is needed.

- Evidence: CMF 0.72, crash types All, severity K;A;B;C; engineering review only.
- Source: CMF Clearinghouse record 267, https://cmfclearinghouse.fhwa.dot.gov/detail.php?facid=267
- Limitation: Adjusted SE is 0.06. This record supports one added left-turn lane, not right-turn lanes or a protected left-turn signal. KABC is not KA only.

### CM034: Replace 8-inch red signal heads with 12-inch heads

Replacing 8-inch red signal heads with 12-inch heads is partially supported for angle crashes. The CMF 0.58 (CMF Clearinghouse 2333) covers angle crashes and all severities, but not exclusively fatal/serious; engineering review is needed to confirm applicability.

- Evidence: CMF 0.58, crash types Angle, severity All; engineering review only.
- Source: CMF Clearinghouse record 2333, https://cmfclearinghouse.fhwa.dot.gov/detail.php?facid=2333
- Limitation: Empirical Bayes study in Winston-Salem, NC. Angle crashes at all severities. Blank area field is not evidence of rural applicability; confirm study setting before use.
