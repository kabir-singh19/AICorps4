## Recommended countermeasures

### CM034: Replace 8-inch red signal heads with 12-inch heads

Replacing 8-inch red signal heads with 12-inch heads is supported for angle crashes, which are frequent at this site. The cited CMF 0.58 (CMF Clearinghouse 2333) is a partial crash-type match and requires engineering review for area setting and severity scope.

- Evidence: CMF 0.58, crash types Angle, severity All; engineering review only.
- Source: CMF Clearinghouse record 2333, https://cmfclearinghouse.fhwa.dot.gov/detail.php?facid=2333
- Limitation: Empirical Bayes study in Winston-Salem, NC. Angle crashes at all severities. Blank area field is not evidence of rural applicability; confirm study setting before use.

### CM015: Provide left-turn lane on one major-road approach

Providing a left-turn lane on one major-road approach addresses left-turn opposing crashes. The cited CMF 0.72 (CMF Clearinghouse 267) is a broader-evidence entry and needs engineering review for geometry, traffic volumes, and severity scope.

- Evidence: CMF 0.72, crash types All, severity K;A;B;C; engineering review only.

- Limitation: Adjusted SE is 0.06. This record supports one added left-turn lane, not right-turn lanes or a protected left-turn signal. KABC is not KA only.
