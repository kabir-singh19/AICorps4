Providing a left-turn lane on one major-road approach addresses the high frequency of left-turn opposing crashes. The cited CMF 0.72 (CMF Clearinghouse 267) is a direct crash-type match for this intersection configuration, but requires engineering review for severity scope and prerequisites.
